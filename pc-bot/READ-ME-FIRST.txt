HOW TO START (Windows)

Do NOT double-click files while the zip is still open.
That puts you in a Temp folder and it will fail.

1. Close this zip window.
2. Go to Downloads.
3. RIGHT-CLICK  MT5-Demo-for-your-PC.zip
4. Click  Extract All...
5. Click  Extract
6. Open the NEW folder (look at the address bar: it must NOT say Temp or .zip)
7. Double-click  DOUBLE-CLICK-ME

It copies itself to:
  C:\Users\YOURNAME\MT5-Demo

I cannot put files on your PC from the website. You have to Extract All once.
